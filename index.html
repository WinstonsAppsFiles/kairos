Kairos Life · Support

See where your time actually goes.

Kairos Life is a time ledger. Chronos keeps the books: every hour you spend, logged like a bank statement. Kairos asks whether those hours are building the life you want.

Common questions

What's the fastest way to log my day?

Tap an activity on the Chronos tab to start a timer, and tap another to switch. If you forget, use Fill on the "unaccounted" card. Every gap can be filled with one tap, or split up with Custom.

What if I genuinely can't remember?

Use Don't remember. That time counts as logged but stays neutral, so it doesn't count as productive or lost. Short in-between moments can be marked as Transition, or all at once with the bulk button in the gap filler.

How are intentions scored?

A day only counts once it has at least an hour logged. Daily intentions show how many days you met them, and weekly ones are compared against where you should be by now. Today counts once it's decided: an "at least" goal you've hit, or an "at most" limit you've gone over.

Notifications aren't showing up.

Check Settings → Notifications → Kairos Life on your iPhone, then make sure the reminders are switched on in the app's own Settings. The evening check only fires if part of your day is still unaccounted for.

How do I add the widget?

Long-press your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Kairos Life. The medium size has one-tap buttons to start a timer.
