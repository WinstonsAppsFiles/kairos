Privacy Policy

Your time stays yours.

Kairos Life is built so that nothing you log ever leaves your iPhone. This page explains exactly what that means.

Effective October 13, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no login.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party code or services that collect data.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.

What the app stores

To work, Kairos Life saves the information you choose to enter: your time entries (start time, end time, category and any notes), your categories, your intentions, your North Star sentence and your reminder settings.

This data is stored locally on your iPhone, inside the app's own private storage, and shared only with Kairos Life's own home-screen widget and Live Activity on the same device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. We don't run any servers, so we couldn't see your data even if we wanted to.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device: an evening gap check and an alert if a timer has run for a long time. These are created and delivered entirely on your iPhone. No notification content is sent over the internet.

Exporting your data

Settings includes an option to export your ledger as a CSV file. The export is only created when you ask for it, and it only goes wherever you choose to share it.

Deleting your data

You can erase all time entries at any time from Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Kairos Life doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
